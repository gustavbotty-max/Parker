# Jonathan Brain Dump Inbox

Purpose: capture voice-note brain dumps, convert them into structured notes, and review them on a schedule for action items and proactive AI opportunities.

## How this should be used
- Default assumption: **all voice notes are brain dumps** unless Jonathan explicitly says `Main session action`.
- After each brain dump, append a structured note under the most relevant tag(s).
- Keep entries concise, date-stamped, and easy to scan.
- Treat this file as an inbox / working memory layer, not polished documentation.

## Review goals
The cron review should:
1. identify action items
2. identify unresolved questions
3. suggest proactive AI help
4. group related thoughts
5. keep suggestions practical, not fluffy
