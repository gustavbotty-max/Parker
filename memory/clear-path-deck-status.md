# Clear Path Pitch Deck — Current Status

_Date: 2026-03-26_

## Purpose
This file captures where the Clear Path pitch deck currently stands so a fresh session or another model can pick up without re-deriving context from chat history.

---

## Current deck concept
The deck is **mission-first**, aimed primarily at churches as early partners.

Core strategic framing:
- churches are the first trust layer
- Clear Path helps churches connect, give, and serve more effectively now
- long-term mission is larger than church giving: the platform is intended to become trusted enough to support money-to-need / support-distribution use cases later

Current phrase direction:
- prefer **Tap to Connect** over **Tap to Give**
- reason: Tap to Connect feels broader, more helpful, and less like "give us money"
- giving becomes one path inside the tap experience, alongside notes, announcements, next steps, etc.

---

The latest working deck was expanded to roughly this flow:

## Current concerns / known weaknesses
### 1. Visual polish is still not where it should be
The structure is better, but the overall design still needs a real taste pass.
Known issues:
- some margins/spacing still feel off
- branding/colors still need stronger cohesion
- some slides still feel template-driven rather than fully intentional
- mobile layout was improved, but may still need refinement

### 2. The deck still needs a genuine design/taste audit
The intent was to use a stronger model (like Opus 4.6) for:
- final taste review
- hierarchy critique
- copy tightening
- slide order critique
- identifying weak or redundant slides

### 3. Visual asset generation was mixed
AI-generated visuals were very inconsistent.
Useful outcomes:
- one usable **Tap to Connect** product mockup
- one acceptable blurred church/community background direction
- Raleigh skyline photo and Durham train photo from Jonathan are better as local identity assets than most generated alternatives

Less useful outcomes:
- AI-generated hero scenes often looked fake or stock-photo-ish
- AI-generated logos/text frequently misspelled words
- conclusion: AI image generation is useful for concept assets, but not reliable for final text/logo treatment

## Product / pitch positioning currently favored
### Phase 1 offering direction
The deck should emphasize:
- beautiful custom giving page
- ACH switch campaign / lower fee drag
- Tap to Connect tools

Package thinking included:
- Starter
- Launch Package
- Ultimate Launch

### Strategic direction
Clear Path should not be presented as:
- just another giving button
- just another church texting product
- just another NFC giveaway gimmick

It should be presented as:
- a trust layer first
- a better church connection/giving product now
- a deeper financial operating layer later

### Competitor framing
Current thinking:
- Clearstream and Overflow are the most relevant early comparison points in the tap/connect/giving space
- Clearstream is a stronger Phase-1 style front-end competitor
- Overflow is potentially a more strategic longer-term category competitor
- Clear Path’s differentiation should be:
  - starts with connection
  - expands into giving trust / transparency
  - grows toward treasury / yield / cards / money deployment

## Design feedback already identified
### What worked
- cover direction improved
- mission/problem flow made more sense
- Tap to Connect concept became stronger than Tap to Give
- local-first framing using Raleigh/Durham imagery felt more authentic

### What still needs review
- Which slide should be the true emotional anchor?
- Are there too many slides explaining the mission and not enough showing clear product mechanics?
- Is the deck trying to do too much at once?
- Does the product feel distinct enough from Clearstream / Overflow?
- Are the visuals premium enough, or still too placeholder-like?
- Do the later slides earn the bigger mission, or just gesture toward it?
