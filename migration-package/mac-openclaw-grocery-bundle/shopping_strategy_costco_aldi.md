**Goal:** Reduce grocery budget by 15-20% through strategic Costco + Aldi shopping

- [ ] **Chobani Greek Yogurt Drinks** (bulk case) – $6.50/unit vs $9.20 WM = **Save $40/trip**

- [ ] **Ground Beef 80/20** (2-3 lb bulk pack) – $4.99/lb vs $6.99 WM = **Save $30-45/trip**
