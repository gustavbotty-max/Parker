Clear Path — Partner Architecture Memo

A detailed internal brief focused on where revenue is generated, what the technical moat is in each phase, what we build vs what we implement, and how the stack evolves from Raleigh launch to mature platform.

Updated: 2026-03-23

Why this memo exists

This version is for partner / architect review. It focuses on:
revenue generation, competitive advantage, build-vs-buy, partner dependencies, and phase-specific technical requirements.

Operating model assumed throughout:

Clear Path is the software orchestrator and rules engine, while regulated partners handle money movement, fund housing/custody, yield products, and card issuance.

Core strategic thesis

What Clear Path is not

  • Not just cheaper giving
  • Not just a crypto wallet experience
  • Not just a social-impact idea without infrastructure

What Clear Path should become

  • A financial operating layer for mission-driven organizations
  • Unifying giving, treasury, restricted-fund traceability, and controlled spend
  • Making money easier to receive, govern, grow, and deploy

Revenue model overview

1. Intake economics

Money in

  • Payment fee share
  • ACH/card economics
  • Conversion economics where applicable

2. Treasury economics

Money held

  • Yield share
  • Treasury spread / conversion economics
  • Premium treasury features

3. Deployment economics

Money out

  • Accelerated payout fee
  • Premium treasury action fee
  • Vendor / recipient speed premium

4. Spend economics

Money deployed via cards

  • Interchange share
  • Premium card controls
  • Program administration revenue

Pricing / Fee Model by Phase

Phase 1 — Trust + Savings

  • No platform setup fee
  • No monthly platform fee for basic adoption
  • Cards / digital wallets: target ~2.2%–2.9% + $0.30
  • ACH / bank transfer: target ~0.0% + $0.30 on the best end, more realistically 0.5%–1.0% + small fixed fee
  • Wire: usually pass-through or admin-fee based for larger transfers
  • Advanced traceability / board-grade reporting: $49–$149/month
  • Optional concierge migration: $250–$1,500 one-time
  • ACH migration incentive: treated as CAC / margin investment

Phase 2 — Treasury + Yield

  • Yield share: 10%–20% of net yield produced
  • Optional premium treasury package: $99–$299/month
  • Advanced treasury reporting: $99–$249/month
  • Advanced governance / approvals: $49–$199/month
  • Accelerated payout: 0.25%–1.00% of accelerated amount

Phase 3 — Cards + Controlled Spend

  • Advanced spend controls: $99–$499/month
  • Physical cards: pass-through or $5–$25/card
  • MCC / policy controls: $49–$249/month
  • Receipt / audit workflow: $49–$149/month
  • Program administration / budgeting: $99–$499/month

Phase 4 — Mission Operating System

  • Program administration fee: $99–$999/month
  • Beneficiary / program reporting: $99–$299/month
  • Advanced donor transparency: $49–$199/month
  • Case-management / support workflow layer: $149–$999/month
  • Interchange + treasury economics: still retained behind the scenes
Cross-phase pricing principle: keep adoption friction low, avoid ugly upfront fees, make standard functionality feel generous, and charge for premium transparency, governance, acceleration, and mission/program complexity.

Final conclusion

The winning version of Clear Path:
Save money first.
Earn trust second.
Turn trust into treasury adoption third.
Turn treasury into deployment economics fourth.
Use proven infrastructure to realize the full mission last.
